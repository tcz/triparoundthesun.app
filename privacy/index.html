Privacy Policy

Trip Around the Sun • Last updated: September 2026

Trip Around the Sun is a free birthday and anniversary reminder app. Your privacy matters to us, and we designed the app so that your data stays under your control.

This Privacy Policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

1. Data Collection

Trip Around the Sun does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to any servers.

  • We do not operate a backend server for your events.
  • We do not collect names, dates, notes, or event information outside your device.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal data.

All information you add to the app remains on your device unless you choose to use iCloud syncing.

2. iCloud Sync

If iCloud is enabled on your device, Trip Around the Sun can sync your events using Apple's iCloud services.

  • Data is stored in your private iCloud container.
  • Apple encrypts and manages this data.
  • We do not have access to your iCloud data.
  • iCloud syncing is optional, and the app works without it.

3. Offline Use

Trip Around the Sun works fully offline. You can add, edit, and view events without an internet connection.

4. Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

  • Notifications are optional; you can turn them off at any time in Settings.
  • No notification content is sent through external servers.

5. Contacts Import

You may choose to import birthdays from your device's Contacts.

  • Contacts access is read-only.
  • Only contacts with birthdays are imported.
  • Contact data is stored locally on your device.
  • Contacts are never uploaded or shared.

6. Screenshot Import

The app can read birthdays from screenshots, for example of Facebook's birthday list.

  • You manually select the screenshots.
  • All processing happens on your device.
  • Screenshots never leave your device.
  • Screenshots are not uploaded, stored, or shared.

7. Gift Ideas

Gift ideas are brainstormed on your device using Apple Intelligence.

  • The names, relationships, and interests you enter never leave your device.
  • Saved ideas are stored locally and, if enabled, in your private iCloud container.

8. Analytics, Tracking, and Advertising

Trip Around the Sun does not use:

  • Analytics tools
  • Crash reporting services
  • Advertising networks
  • Tracking technologies
  • Advertising identifiers (including IDFA)

We do not track your activity in the app.

9. Accounts and Identity

  • The app does not require an account.
  • There are no logins, usernames, or passwords.
  • No personal identifiers are created or stored.

10. Children's Privacy

Trip Around the Sun is suitable for users of all ages.

  • The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
  • No data is transmitted to external servers.

11. Changes to This Policy

If the app's data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly. Any changes will apply only going forward.
